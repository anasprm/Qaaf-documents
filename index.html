What documents are needed for Emirates ID renewal at Qaaf Documents?

For Emirates ID renewal at Qaaf Documents, you typically need:

  • Original passport (with a copy)
  • Current Emirates ID card (original, if not expired)
  • Valid UAE residence visa page copy
  • Recent passport-sized photograph (white background)
  • For children under 15: original birth certificate

Requirements may vary slightly depending on your visa category (employment, investor, student, Golden Visa, etc.). Our team will verify your specific requirements during consultation — WhatsApp us on +971 52 111 3478 to confirm before you visit.

How long does MOFA attestation take in Dubai?

Standard MOFA attestation in Dubai typically takes 2–5 working days through official MOFA channels. Qaaf Documents also offers an express MOFA attestation service that can be completed within 24–48 hours for urgent cases (subject to document type and availability).

Factors that may affect processing time include: the type of document (educational, personal, or commercial), the issuing country, and the current MOFA processing load. Contact us for a real-time estimate specific to your document.

  • Visa stamping coordination

Our experienced PRO team manages all submissions on your behalf, so you can focus on welcoming your family while we handle the paperwork.

What is the difference between legal translation and normal translation in the UAE?

In the UAE, legal translation (certified translation) is fundamentally different from general translation:

  • Legal Translation: Performed by a translator officially licensed and registered with the UAE Ministry of Justice. The translated document carries an official Ministry stamp and the translator's license number, making it legally recognized by all UAE courts, government entities, embassies, and financial institutions.
  • Normal Translation: Carried out by any bilingual individual or translation agency without official certification. Suitable for general informational purposes only — not accepted for any official UAE government or embassy submission.

All legal translations at Qaaf Documents are performed by our Ministry of Justice-certified translators and carry full official validity across the UAE.
